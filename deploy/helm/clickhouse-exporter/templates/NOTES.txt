ClickHouse exporter has been installed.

Metrics:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "clickhouse-exporter.fullname" . }} 9116:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl http://localhost:9116/metrics

Prometheus:
  - point the scrape config at service {{ include "clickhouse-exporter.fullname" . }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  - or enable serviceMonitor.enabled=true if the Prometheus Operator CRD is installed

Configuration:
  - ConfigMap: {{ include "clickhouse-exporter.fullname" . }}-config
  - DSN secret: {{ include "clickhouse-exporter.secretName" . }} (key {{ .Values.clickhouse.secretKey }})

Remember to grant the exporter user SELECT on system.* and on every monitored
database; otherwise system.parts and friends are filtered by table access.
